By submitting a pull request, you agree:

1. Your contribution is licensed under Apache-2.0
2. You retain responsibility for your own liability regarding the code
3. You warrant the contribution doesn't knowingly infringe third-party IP
4. You will not submit:
   - Captured seed/key material
   - Proprietary calibration binaries
   - VIN data or PII
   - Exploit logic designed to bypass security gates

Maintainers may reject contributions for any reason, including but not
limited to regulatory concerns, safety risks, or potential misuse.
